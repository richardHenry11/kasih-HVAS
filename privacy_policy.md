# Privacy Policy for HVAS

**Last updated: September 2026**

PT. Cakrawala Bima Instrument ("we", "us", or "our") operates the HVAS mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

## Information Collection and Use

To provide and improve our service, the HVAS App requests certain permissions and accesses specific device features. We **do not** collect, store, or transmit any personal identifying information (PII) to our servers without your explicit consent.

### 1. Bluetooth and Location Data
The App requires Bluetooth permissions (`BLUETOOTH`, `BLUETOOTH_ADMIN`, `BLUETOOTH_SCAN`, `BLUETOOTH_CONNECT`) to communicate with HVAS hardware devices. 

Due to Android OS requirements, accessing Bluetooth scan results requires Location permissions (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`, `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`). 
- **How we use it:** We use location permissions **strictly** to scan for nearby Bluetooth devices. 
- **Data storage:** We do not track, store, or share your physical location. Location data is processed locally on your device for the sole purpose of establishing a Bluetooth connection.

### 2. Storage Data
The App requests file storage permissions (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`).
- **How we use it:** These permissions are used exclusively to save configuration data, export logs, or generate report files locally on your device.
- **Data privacy:** We do not access your personal files, photos, or documents. We do not transmit your local files to our servers.

## Data Security
The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. All communication between the App and the HVAS hardware is done locally via Bluetooth.

## Children's Privacy
Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
